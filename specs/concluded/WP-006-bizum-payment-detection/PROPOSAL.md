# Bizum Payment Detection

The organiser still settles every Bizum payment in Pachanguero by hand, although the bank already holds who paid what and when (the PoC read 412 Bizums from 63 senders in 13 months through Enable Banking).
Integrate the Revolut/Bizum feed into Pachanguero for real: read the incoming Bizums on a schedule, match each one to a player's debts and settle them automatically, with every payment traceable to its Bizum and correctable by the organiser.
Removes the most repetitive admin chore of a game week, with no manual work, and keeps debts accurate, using what WP-005 already learned about the feed, its limits and its renewal.
