# Convocatoria Is The Final List

The match lifecycle makes the organiser paste a separate final list to record who played, though the confirmed convocatoria already says it; the paste duplicates it, and last-minute changes have nowhere to go before kickoff.
Make the confirmed convocatoria the final list: editable by hand until the game, with who played derived from the difference between the apuntados and the convocatoria. The Claros/Oscuros paste stays as a later, optional, team-only phase.
Removes a duplicated paste, models the real order (apuntados, convocatoria, edit, play), and reverses WP-001's rule that only a pasted final list records played.
